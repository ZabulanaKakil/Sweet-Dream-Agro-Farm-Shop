About us

Sweet Dream Agro Farm grows and supplies plants, pots, seeds and garden essentials for homes and communities across Bangladesh — rooted in the dream of a greener country.

From balcony herbs to garden fruit trees, we hand-select stock and keep care notes clear so your plants thrive after they leave our farm.
